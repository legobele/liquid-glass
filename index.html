Glass floats.
Content flows.

Apple's rule, honored here: Liquid Glass lives only in the floating functional layer — toolbars, tab bars, docks, sheets. Everything you read sits on standard materials. Scroll: the aurora slides under the toolbar and the tab bar, and the glass bends it.

Content layer · standard materials, never glass

Cards don't get glass

The HIG is explicit: putting Liquid Glass in the content layer muddies the hierarchy. Cards, lists and app backgrounds use plain frosted materials instead.
